About Me

My name is C'lester "Ricky" Jackson III, i was born in the year 1990 in New Orleans, Louisiana. I found joy in discovery but with those discoveries i wanted to create my own version of them, this has been a virtue of mine since a child. Creating and manipulating structured systems has always intrigue me. In middle school i found joy in reading recipes and putting my own spin on it to create it as my own, as i progressed through primary school i chose arts and technology as first options in the elective pool. Others flocked to driver's ed, theater, and sports, while i choose visual art, music, and computer classes.

In my freshmen year of high school soon after the birth of social media, came myspace. All the kids were on myspace. I choose web Development in more primitive technological state, as software was still growing and websites were mainly still static, i did my best and enjoyed all of it even using notepad as a IDE to create webpages. As simple as they were i didnt realize at the time how much code it needed and the rules or syntax required to make a dream/design a reality. Although i didnt follow up with more web or software classes, i did enjoy myspace invitation to find code online to create personalized webpages for friends and family to check out. During the last year or two of High school my passion for computers and how they work swayed into language and the culinary arts.
