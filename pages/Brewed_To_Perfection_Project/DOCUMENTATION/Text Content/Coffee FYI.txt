Coffee FYI

Espresso: a concentrated shot of coffee made by forcing pressurized hot water through finely ground coffee beans.
Macchiato: a shot of espresso "marked" with a small amount of frothed milk.
Cortado: a shot of espresso topped with an equal amount of milk.
Cappuccino: a combination of espresso, steamed milk, and frothed milk.
Latte: a combination of espresso and steamed milk, with a thin layer of frothed milk on top.
Mocha: a variant of latte made with chocolate syrup or cocoa powder added to the drink.
Caffè au Lait: a coffee drink made with brewed coffee and scalded milk, with a 1:1 ratio of coffee to milk.

Types of Milk:

Whole Milk: rich and creamy, with a high fat content (around 3.5%)
2% Milk: a blend of whole milk and skim milk, with a lower fat content (around 2%)
Skim Milk: a low-fat milk with almost no fat content (around 0.5%)
Half-and-Half: a mixture of milk and cream, with a high fat content (around 10-12%)
Buttermilk: a tangy and creamy milk with a slightly acidic taste
Alternative Milks:

Almond Milk: a plant-based milk made from almonds and water, low in calories and rich in vitamins
Soy Milk: a plant-based milk made from soybeans and water, high in protein and isoflavones
Coconut Milk: a creamy and rich milk made from coconut meat and water, high in saturated fat
Oat Milk: a plant-based milk made from oats and water, low in calories and rich in fiber
Rice Milk: a hypoallergenic milk made from rice and water, low in calories and rich in carbohydrates
Cashew Milk: a creamy and rich milk made from cashews and water, low in calories and rich in magnesium
Hemp Milk: a plant-based milk made from hemp seeds and water, high in protein and essential fatty acids
Flax Milk: a plant-based milk made from flaxseeds and water, low in calories and rich in omega-3 fatty acids
Macadamia Nut Milk: a creamy and rich milk made from macadamia nuts and water, low in calories and rich in healthy fats
Other Options:

Lactose-Free Milk: a milk that has had the lactose (milk sugar) removed, suitable for those with lactose intolerance
A2 Milk: a type of milk that contains only A2 beta-casein protein, which may be easier to digest for some people
Goat's Milk: a type of milk that is similar to cow's milk but with a slightly different nutritional profile
Note: This is not an exhaustive list, and there may be other types of milk and alternative milks available depending on the region and personal preferences.
